# Predictopoly

> [!WARNING]
> **Market updates paused since 13 November 2026.** Live-mode questions and
> prices are no longer refreshed and may be out of date. Past-market practice
> remains available. See [project status](#project-status).

Practice probabilistic forecasting with real Polymarket questions.

**[Try Predictopoly →](https://predictopoly.com)**

How often do the things you call 70% likely actually happen? Predictopoly
helps you explore that by making predictions, checking outcomes, and tracking
your calibration over time. Choose a question, estimate the probability of
YES, and compare your forecast with the outcome and the market's estimate.
Free to use, with no account or money required.

## How it works

- **Live markets (paused):** the saved market snapshot is still accessible,
  but questions and prices are no longer refreshed and may be stale. Existing
  open predictions and history remain in your browser.
- **Past markets:** practice on resolved questions and reveal the outcome
  immediately after submitting your probability.
- **Choose your subjects:** use deck presets or filter by category,
  subcategory, and market volume. Subjects include politics, economics,
  technology, crypto, sports, culture, and science.
- **Review your forecasts:** see your prediction history, a calibration
  chart, scores by category, and comparisons with market probabilities.
  Stats can show past-market practice, resolved live predictions, or both.

## Scoring

- **Brier score:** squared error between your probability and the outcome
  (`1` for YES, `0` for NO). Lower is better.
- **Log loss:** another forecast score that penalizes confident mistakes
  more strongly. Lower is better.
- **Points:** a game score combining your Brier score with a bounded bonus
  or penalty for doing better or worse than the market, where a comparison
  price is available.

Past-market comparisons use the earliest available price snapshot from
30, 7, or 1 day before resolution. Live predictions store the market price
from the loaded dataset when you submit; this is a snapshot, not a live quote.
Per-question percentiles can include a synthetic baseline generated by the
[seed script](scripts/06_seed_percentile.py), alongside submitted predictions.

Your history, open predictions, and preferences are stored in your browser's
local storage. They do not sync between devices, and clearing site data
removes them. The app also sends submitted predictions and a random session
identifier to its API for aggregate statistics and per-question percentiles.

## Data and updates

Questions, resolution data, and market prices come from Polymarket. The
repository includes source data in Parquet and JSONL format and generated
JSON bundles used by the website.

Before the pause, the [refresh workflow](.github/workflows/refresh-active.yml)
ran daily. It promoted newly resolved markets into the past dataset, fetched
active markets, classified new questions, and exported the web bundles.
Its schedule remains in the workflow file, but the workflow is disabled in
GitHub Actions and does not run automatically.

The canonical categories and subcategories live in
[`scripts/_taxonomy.py`](scripts/_taxonomy.py), shared by the classifiers and
exporters. [`data/proposed_taxonomy.md`](data/proposed_taxonomy.md) is the
original proposal, not the current taxonomy.

## Local preview

The frontend is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with generated data included
in the repository. To preview it with Python 3, run from the repository root:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory web
```

Open [localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000). This serves the frontend and
bundled questions; resolution checks and percentile statistics require the
Cloudflare Pages Functions backend. Local history and past-market scoring
work without that backend.
